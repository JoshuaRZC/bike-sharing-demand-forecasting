This portion of the assignment describes the requirements for the project presentation. 

For your final project presentation, you should create some slides describing the results of your project and record a 17-20 minute video. Please do not go over 20 minutes. The slides can include some of your code, but should be more of an overview of the problem and its significance rather than a walkthrough of a jupyter notebook. Please include the following information:

- Introduction. What is the problem and why does it matter? Assume your audience is technically competent but not expert in your specific domain. Give enough background that they can follow your reasoning. Do not assume any background in physics, neuroscience, finance, climate science, civil engineering, etc. (whatever your domain area is). 
- Data and methods. What data did you use and what did you do with it? This should include enough detail that someone could evaluate your choices, but it should not be a deep dive into your code. Equations and diagrams are helpful.
- Results. What did you find? Present your results clearly, with appropriate figures. Interpret them in the context of your original question.
- Conclusion. What do your results mean? What are the limitations? What would you do next with more time?

 

You are not limited to a certain number of slides, just keep the timing at 17-20 minutes and you should be fine. You can use Zoom to record, or you can Screen Record yourself any way you find convenient. Your slides should have some visuals to accompany your results. Upload a link to your video via Zoom, or upload your video to Google Drive and link to it here. Please make sure it is accessible to course staff.
